How to use this tool

  1. Type or paste your draft into the text box. The counts update while you edit, so you can check a headline, essay, script, caption, or form response without submitting anything.
  2. Check Words for a word limit or Characters for a character limit. The Without spaces number excludes all whitespace, including line breaks and tabs.
  3. Use the sentence count to see how your draft is divided. Reading time is calculated at 200 words per minute and rounded up to the nearest second.
  4. Use Copy text to return the edited draft to your document, or Clear text to start over. Copy any draft you want to keep before leaving the page.

Features and privacy

Text analysis runs in the browser, and ToolVibe does not upload or save the draft. Modern browsers use their built-in word, sentence, and grapheme segmentation, which handles more writing systems and combined characters than splitting solely on spaces. Older browsers use simpler counting rules.

Character counts include visible punctuation and whitespace. Many emoji and combining accents count as one displayed character in modern browsers. A destination app may count UTF-16 units, bytes, or a custom sequence used by its own limits. When a destination has a strict cutoff, confirm the final result in that field.

Frequently asked questions

How are hyphenated words counted?

The result depends on your browser’s word segmentation. A word processor may handle hyphens, punctuation, and apostrophes differently. Enter a short example to see how this browser counts it.

Does an unfinished sentence count?

Text that contains words or numbers can count as a sentence even when the final punctuation is missing. Abbreviations and unusual punctuation may influence boundaries.

Is reading time the same as speaking time?

Reading time is an estimate for silent reading at a fixed pace. If you are rehearsing a speech, time yourself aloud to account for pauses and emphasis.
