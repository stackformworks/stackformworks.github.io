Merge PDF

Combine several PDFs into one file. Your documents stay in your browser.

File order

    Download merged PDF

    How to combine PDF files

    1. Select two or more PDFs. You can add files in more than one batch.
    2. Use Move up and Move down to arrange the files. Each file's pages stay in their original order.
    3. Choose Merge PDFs, then download the combined file.

    What the merged PDF keeps

    The merger copies pages in their original sizes. For example, you can put a cover sheet first, a report second, and the appendices last. Scanned PDFs work as page images. Password-protected files must be unlocked first. Interactive form fields, bookmarks, and digital signatures may be lost.

    To rotate, remove, or rearrange individual pages, open the result in the PDF Editor.
