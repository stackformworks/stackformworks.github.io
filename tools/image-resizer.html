How to resize an image

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP image. ToolVibe shows its width and height after opening it.
  2. Choose a preset or enter dimensions in pixels. For example, enter 1080 × 1080 for a square post.
  3. Pick a resize mode and file format, then select Resize image. Check the preview and the actual output dimensions before downloading.

Pick the right resize mode

Fit within bounds keeps the whole image and fits it inside your requested width and height. If the shapes differ, one output dimension will be smaller. Center crop fills the exact size by trimming the edges around the center. Stretch also gives the exact size, but can change how people or objects look.

Frequently asked questions

How do I keep transparency?

Choose PNG or WebP with a transparent source. JPG fills transparent areas with white.

Can I improve image quality by enlarging it?

Enlarging an image gives it more pixels. Existing details may look softer at the new size.

Where is the image processed?

Your browser opens, resizes, and exports it. The image is kept on your device. The result is a still image, so animated WebP files export as a single frame.
